Saving

Save recipes from any website in two taps

Share any page to Saucier from Safari and it keeps the parts you came for — ingredients, method, servings, timings, nutrition — and leaves the ads, the pop-ups and the thousand words about someone's holiday behind.

Paste a link instead if you prefer. Saucier spots a recipe URL already on your clipboard and offers to save it before you have typed anything.

Ask Saucier

An AI cooking assistant that runs on your iPhone

Ask Saucier what you can make with what is in the fridge, how much butter the recipe wanted, what to use instead of buttermilk, or what 200 g is in cups. It answers from your own library and from the recipe in front of you — not from the open web.

Turn Voice on and it listens, answers out loud and moves the recipe on a step, so nothing needs touching between putting a pan down and picking it up again. It is built on Apple Intelligence and runs on your device, which is why it works on a plane and why your questions never reach a server.

Cooking

Cook mode, with timers that keep running

Tap Make It and the recipe becomes one step at a time, in type you can read from across the counter, on a screen that will not dim on you mid-fold.

Start a timer from a step and it carries on in the background, shows up on your Lock Screen as a Live Activity and tells you when it is done — even if you have left the app to answer a message. Several can run at once, and the On Now list keeps them all in one place.

Your library

Everything you cook, in one place

A recipe you cannot find again is a recipe you did not save. Saucier is built around getting back to things quickly.

Switching

Bring the recipes you already have

Years of saved recipes are the reason most people stay in an app they have stopped liking. Saucier reads Paprika's .paprikarecipes export directly — titles, ingredients, directions, notes, source links, photos and categories.

It also tells you, before anything is imported, which Paprika fields it has nowhere to put. Finding out months later that your ratings stayed behind is not a good way to find out.

Privacy

No account. No ads. No tracking.

Saucier has no server of its own. Your recipes are stored on your device and synced through your private iCloud account, which means nobody at Saucier can read them — there is nowhere for them to look.

There is no analytics SDK, no advertising network and no sign-up. The Ask Saucier assistant runs on your iPhone or iPad using Apple Intelligence, so even your questions stay with you.
