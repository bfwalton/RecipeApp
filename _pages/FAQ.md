faq:
  - q: Is Saucier free?
    a: >-
      Yes. Free to download, with no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads
      and no account to create. Saucier is a side project built in spare time
      rather than a business. If that ever changes, recipes you have already
      saved will stay available to you.
  - q: What do I need to run it?
    a: >-
      An iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or later. Ask Saucier additionally needs a
      device with Apple Intelligence available and switched on. Meal planning
      works on any supported device. On-device nutrition estimates need Apple
      Intelligence; USDA lookups need a connection.
  - q: Is there a Mac, Android or web version?
    a: >-
      No. Saucier is iPhone and iPad only, and there is no plan for other
      platforms at the moment. If you cook from a Windows laptop or an Android
      phone, another app will serve you better.
  - q: Where are my recipes stored?
    a: >-
      On your device, and synced through your own private iCloud account.
      Saucier has no server, so your recipes are never uploaded to one and
      nobody at Saucier can read them.
  - q: Does Saucier track me or show ads?
    a: >-
      No. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising network and no third-party
      tracking of any kind in the app.
  - q: Which recipe websites work?
    a: >-
      Effectively all of them. Most publish machine-readable recipe data that
      Saucier reads directly. Where a page publishes none — an Instagram caption
      written as a story, say — Apple Intelligence reads the prose on your
      device and produces the ingredients and steps.
  - q: Can I save from Instagram and TikTok?
    a: >-
      Yes, via the share sheet. Saucier reads the recipe out of the caption. It
      does not transcribe a video's audio.
  - q: Does Ask Saucier send my questions to a server?
    a: >-
      No. It runs on Apple Intelligence's on-device model, so your questions and
      your recipes stay on your device and it works with no signal. Voice input
      uses iOS speech recognition, which runs on device wherever your device
      supports it.
  - q: Does nutrition estimation send recipe data to USDA?
    a: >-
      Only if you choose USDA FoodData Central as the source. Saucier sends
      ingredient names for lookup directly from your device to USDA, along with
      your API key or the shared demo key. The default Apple Intelligence source
      works on device and does not send those lookups anywhere.
  - q: Can I import from Paprika?
    a: >-
      Yes. Export a `.paprikarecipes` file and open it in Saucier. Ingredients,
      directions, notes, source links, photos, servings, nutrition, prep and cook
      times and categories come across. Star ratings and difficulty do not, and
      Saucier tells you so before importing.
  - q: Can I get my recipes back out?
    a: >-
      Yes. Export the whole library as a `.sauce` file from settings at any
      time, and share individual recipes as files or links.
  - q: Does it work offline?
    a: >-
      Your library, meal plan, grocery list, cook mode, timers and search work
      offline. The assistant works offline on devices with Apple Intelligence.
      Saving a new web recipe and looking up nutrition through USDA need a
      connection.
  - q: Is there a meal planner or a pantry?
    a: >-
      There is a weekly meal plan and an in-app grocery list built from it. You
      can send unticked grocery items to Reminders. There is no pantry tracker,
      and the meal plan does not sync between devices yet.
  - q: Can I share recipes with the person I cook with?
    a: >-
      Yes, two ways. Share a recipe over iCloud so that edits show up for both of
      you, or send it as a file or a link to anyone.
  - q: Why is it called Saucier?
    a: >-
      A saucier is the cook in a brigade who runs the sauces — the station that
      has to keep several things going at once and taste everything. It seemed
      like the right job title for an app whose main trick is keeping track of
      what is on.
  - q: I have an idea or a bug to report
    a: >-
      Please send it to <a href="mailto:help@saucier-app.com">help@saucier-app.com</a>.
      It is one person reading that inbox, and feedback genuinely changes what
      gets built next.

Saucier is a free recipe app for iPhone and iPad. It saves recipes from any
website, Instagram or TikTok, keeps them in a library that syncs over your own
iCloud account, and reads them back to you while you cook.
