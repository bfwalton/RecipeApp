## Put your recipes on the calendar

Choose a saved recipe for breakfast, lunch, dinner or a snack on any day of the
week. Set the servings for that meal without changing the original recipe, and
move or remove it when plans change. The Week tab sits beside Recipes and Ask
Saucier, so the plan is easy to reach while you cook.

## Shop for the whole week

The grocery list gathers ingredients from every planned meal. It scales amounts
where the recipe has a serving count, combines matching items, and groups them
by aisle. Saucier keeps unlike units separate rather than guessing that cups
and grams mean the same thing.

Tick items as you shop. You can share the list as text or send the unticked
items to Reminders. The ticks are for this shopping trip; the plan remains
ready for the next one.

## See what an estimate is based on

Saucier can estimate calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat for a recipe.
This is a **beta** feature: amounts in recipes are often approximate, and some
ingredients cannot be matched. The estimate shows its ingredient coverage and
source rather than presenting a single number as a fact.

Nutrition estimates use the on-device model where Apple Intelligence is
available. You can also choose USDA FoodData Central with your own free API key
in settings. The weekly plan and grocery list do not require nutrition to be
turned on.

Your meal plan stays on this device for now. Saved recipes still sync through
your own iCloud account.
