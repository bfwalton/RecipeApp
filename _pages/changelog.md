## Version 1.5

*Ask Saucier, plan the week, and keep cooking timers close.*

**The week ahead**

- Plan breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks from the recipes you have saved.
- Set servings for each meal, then turn the whole week into one grocery list
  grouped by aisle. Tick items off, share the list or send unticked items to
  Reminders.
- See approximate calories and macros for a recipe with a new nutrition beta.
  It shows which ingredients contributed and where the estimate came from.
- Recipes, Week and Ask Saucier now have their own tabs.

**Ask Saucier**

- A new assistant you can ask about a recipe while you cook — substitutions,
  timings, what a step means, or what you could make with what you have.
- Answers come back as cards rather than walls of text: tap an ingredient to
  tick it off, see a step on its own, convert an amount, or scale the recipe for
  more people.
- Talk to it hands-free. It listens, answers out loud, and listens again.
- Everything runs on your device. Nothing you ask leaves your phone.

**Cook mode**

- Times and temperatures inside a step are now tappable — tap a time to set a
  timer for it.
- Ingredients mentioned in a step are highlighted and link to your checklist.

**Timers**

- Timers are visible everywhere now, not only inside cook mode, with a new
  **On Now** screen listing everything you have on the go.

**iPhone Duo**

- Built for both displays: the library and a recipe side by side when open, and
  controls on the side bar when closed.
- The app now rotates, for the poses that need it.

**Fixes**

- "1 1/2 hours" no longer sets a two hour timer.
- Recipe names with an `&` in them no longer show as `&amp;`.
- Adding an ingredient to your grocery list now confirms it worked.

## Version 1.4

*Our biggest release yet.*

- Ground-up rewrite of the Saucier interface.
- Drastically improved URL parser for most websites.
- Improved recipe search.
- Improved performance.
- Widgets.
- Spotlight search.

## Version 1.3

*Interface updates and iCloud sync.*

- Added iCloud sync.
- Interface updates to improve the experience.

## Version 1.1

*Our first update.*

- Edit your saved recipe images: add from the camera roll, or pick one of the
  images found on the recipe website.
- Added folders to keep your recipes organised.
- Added a new way to share recipes with friends — export a saved recipe as a
  `.sauce` file.
- Added a setting to confirm before deletion.
- Automatically detect a recipe link on your clipboard, for easier saving.
- Added the ability to send ingredients to your Reminders grocery list.
