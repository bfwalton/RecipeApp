If you are reading this, you probably already have a Paprika library and a
reason to be looking around. The honest version is below, including the parts
where Paprika is the better choice.

## First: your recipes will survive the move

Saucier reads Paprika's `.paprikarecipes` export directly. Ingredients,
directions, your notes, source links, photos, servings, nutrition, prep and cook
times, and categories all come across. **Star ratings and difficulty do not**,
and Saucier shows you that during the review step, before anything is imported.

[Full detail on importing →]({{ '/import-recipes/' | relative_url }})

## Where Saucier is different

### It is free, with no subscription

No purchase, no tier, no ads, no account to create. Saucier is a side project,
not a business with a revenue target. That is also the reason for some of the
gaps further down this page — it is a fair trade to be upfront about.

### It has an assistant that runs on your phone

Ask Saucier what you can make with what is in the fridge, how much butter the
recipe wanted, what to use instead of buttermilk, or how to scale it for six.
It answers from **your own library**, out loud if you want, and it runs on
Apple Intelligence on your device — so it works with no signal and your
questions are never transmitted anywhere.

[How Ask Saucier works →]({{ '/ai-recipe-assistant/' | relative_url }})

### It can read recipes that were never written as recipes

Clipping from a food blog is a solved problem; most sites publish structured
recipe data and any decent app reads it. Instagram and TikTok captions do not.
Saucier uses the on-device model to pull the ingredients and steps out of a
caption written as a story.

[Saving from Instagram and TikTok →]({{ '/save-recipes-from-instagram-tiktok/' | relative_url }})

### Cook mode is built for hands you cannot use

One step at a time, a screen that stays awake, timers that keep running in the
background and show up on the Lock Screen as Live Activities, and voice control
so you never touch the phone mid-recipe.

[More about cook mode →]({{ '/hands-free-cooking/' | relative_url }})

### There is no account and no server

Your library lives on your device and syncs through your own iCloud, so there is
no Saucier account to make, no password to lose and no company database holding
your recipes. There is no analytics SDK and no ad network either.

## Where Paprika is still the better choice

This is the part comparison pages usually leave out.

- **You cook from more than Apple devices.** Saucier is iPhone and iPad only.
  Paprika covers platforms Saucier has no answer for at all. If you want your
  recipes on a Windows laptop or an Android phone, stop reading — stay with
  Paprika.
- **You rely on meal planning.** Saucier has no meal calendar. Paprika does.
- **You rely on pantry tracking.** Saucier has none.
- **You want a grocery list inside the app.** Saucier sends ingredients to your
  Reminders grocery list rather than keeping its own, which is tidy if you
  already live in Reminders and a downgrade if you do not.
- **You want a mature, long-supported, commercially funded app.** Paprika has
  been around for years with a company behind it. Saucier is one person's side
  project. That cuts both ways and you should weigh it.

## The short version

If you cook on Apple devices, want the saving and the cooking to be excellent,
and do not need a meal planner or a pantry, Saucier is likely to suit you — and
it costs nothing to find out, because your Paprika library imports in one file
and your old app is still sitting there if you change your mind.

If you are cross-platform, or your week runs on a meal calendar, Paprika is the
better tool and you should keep it.
