## Saving recipes

- **Share sheet** — a **Save to Saucier** action in every app that can share a
  link, so saving from Safari, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit or Messages takes two
  taps without leaving the app you were in.
- **Paste a link** — type or paste a URL into the app. A partial address like
  `cooking.blog/pesto` works as well as a full one.
- **Clipboard detection** — Saucier notices a recipe link already on your
  clipboard and offers to save it before you have typed anything.
- **Structured data parsing** — most food sites publish machine-readable recipe
  data. Saucier reads it directly, which is why saving is instant.
- **AI extraction for everything else** — where a page has no structured recipe,
  Apple Intelligence reads the prose on your device and writes the ingredient
  list and steps. This is what makes Instagram and TikTok captions work.
- **Reload from website** — pull a fresh copy if the original has been corrected,
  and cancel mid-reload if you change your mind.
- **Add by hand** — type a recipe in, including from a cookbook or a card. End a
  line with a colon to start a section like *For the sauce:*.

## Ask Saucier

- **On-device assistant** built on Apple Intelligence. Nothing is sent anywhere,
  and it works with no signal.
- **Answers from your library** — "what can I make with eggs?" searches your own
  recipes by ingredient and by name.
- **Answers in cards, not prose** — tap an ingredient in an answer to tick it
  off, see a single step on its own, or start cooking a result.
- **Substitutions** with ratios and an honest note about what it changes.
- **Scaling** — "make it for six" rescales the amounts.
- **Conversions** — grams to ounces, and a straight answer about the conversions
  that depend on the ingredient.
- **Nutrition** — ask about nutrition published with a recipe; a separate beta
  estimate can calculate approximate calories and macros from its ingredients.
- **Voice mode** — it listens, answers out loud, and listens again.

## Cooking

- **Cook mode** — one step at a time, large type, screen stays awake.
- **Tappable times and temperatures** — tap a time inside a step to start a
  timer for it.
- **Highlighted ingredients** — ingredients mentioned in a step link to your
  checklist, and tick off as you go.
- **Background timers** that keep running when you leave the app, with
  notifications that take you back to the exact recipe and step.
- **Live Activities** — time remaining on the Lock Screen.
- **Several at once** — multiple timers, and more than one recipe on the go.
- **On Now** — one screen listing everything currently cooking, reachable from
  anywhere in the app.
- **Grocery list** — send one ingredient or a whole recipe to your Reminders
  grocery list, with a button that confirms it worked.

[More on cook mode →]({{ '/hands-free-cooking/' | relative_url }})

## Meal planning and groceries

- **Week tab** — put saved recipes into breakfast, lunch, dinner or snack slots.
- **Servings per meal** — plan for the people eating that day without editing
  the original recipe.
- **One grocery list** — gather and scale the ingredients from the week's meals,
  combine matching items and group them by aisle.
- **Shop in Saucier** — tick items off, share the list as text, or send unticked
  items to Reminders.
- **Nutrition estimates in beta** — approximate calories and macros with
  ingredient coverage and the source shown alongside the result. Apple
  Intelligence or your own USDA FoodData Central key can provide estimates.

[More on meal planning →]({{ '/meal-planning/' | relative_url }})

## Your library

- **Search by name or ingredient**, so "the one with the anchovies" is findable.
- **Spotlight** — your recipes appear in system search from the Home Screen.
- **Categories and folders**, with one-tap filtering.
- **Hidden recipes** for things you would rather not have on the front page.
- **Home Screen widgets** showing what you cooked recently.
- **iCloud sync** across iPhone and iPad, through your own account.
- **Shared recipes** over iCloud, so edits show up for everyone you cook with.

## Editing

- **Edit every line** — ingredients, steps, servings, nutrition, notes.
- **Re-parsed on save** so edited amounts still scale and convert correctly.
- **Sections** — group ingredients and steps under their own headings.
- **Bulk text editing** for pasting a whole recipe in at once.
- **Photos** — use your own, pick another from the source page, or crop.
- **Notes** for what you would do differently next time.
- **Drafts are safe** — the editor keeps your changes until you save, and asks
  before discarding.

## Sharing, import and export

- **Import from Paprika** — `.paprikarecipes` and `.paprikarecipe` exports.
- **Import and export `.sauce` files** — Saucier's own format, backward
  compatible with every previous version.
- **Share a recipe** as a file or as its original link.
- **Reviewed imports** — you see what came across, and what has nowhere to go,
  before anything is written.

## The app itself

- **Recipes, Week and Ask tabs** keep the three main areas one tap away.
- **iPhone and iPad**, including side-by-side layouts on larger displays.
- **iPhone Duo** — library and recipe together when open, controls on the side
  bar when closed.
- **Alternate app icons.**
- **Dark mode**, properly.
- **Dynamic Type** — tested at the largest accessibility sizes, where controls
  stack rather than truncate.
- **VoiceOver labels** on every icon-only control.
- **Reduce Motion** honoured.
- **Works offline** — everything except saving something new from the web.

## What Saucier does not do

Worth saying plainly, so you do not install it and go looking:

- No pantry tracking.
- No Mac, Windows, Android or web version — iPhone and iPad only.
- The weekly meal plan stays on the device where you make it; recipes still
  sync over iCloud.
- No recipe discovery feed. Saucier keeps the recipes you bring it.

If cross-platform access or a pantry is central to how you cook, there are apps
that will serve you better, and
[this page says so in more detail]({{ '/paprika-alternative/' | relative_url }}).
